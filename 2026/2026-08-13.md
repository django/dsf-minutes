## Ongoing business

### Executive Director Hiring Update
- The Board met in advance of this meeting to discuss, plan, draft, and announce the [Call for Applicants](https://www.djangoproject.com/weblog/2026/aug/06/call-for-applicants-for-a-django-executive-directo/) for the Django Executive Director.

### Sponsorships
- The Board discussions with Oracle resulted in securing Platinum sponsorship.
- The Board worked with JetBrains to finalize the second part of the fundraising event and prepare for the release of the 2026 Django Developers Survey.


### djangogigs.com
- [djangogigs.com](http://djangogigs.com) has been transferred to the DSF and is now live at [github.com/django/djangogigs.com.](https://github.com/django/djangogigs.com)


### DEP 0019 — Technical Governance for the Django Project
- The Steering Council is pending moving DEP 0019 - Technical Governance for the Django project (formerly DEP 0018) into an acceptance state.
- The [full diff](https://github.com/django/deps/compare/3238164583c83b434fb7f7a183d2a8b3f7a22b7c...637cd181d4447492e028cd91e5afa5366ba51dcd) is available here.


### DSF Fellow Sabbatical Policy
- A draft of the DSF Fellow Sabbatical Policy was created by the board members.
- Motion Passed: RESOLVED, that the Django Software Foundation Board adopts the DSF Fellow Sabbatical Policy as presented, establishing paid sabbatical accrual, eligibility, usage, and provisions for Board-approved unpaid sabbatical for DSF Fellows.


### Bylaws Change for Voting Membership
- Tom Carrick to follow up on the proposed bylaws change.
- The Board discussed whether to vote on the proposed change so that it can proceed to a membership vote.
- The Board discussed keeping the forum open for public comments for a defined period, similar to the process used for adopting the Code of Conduct, before holding the final vote.
- The forum post has been open for approximately one month.


### Security Team Working Group
- The Django Software Foundation Board approves the [Security Team Working Group charter](https://github.com/tim-schilling/dsf-working-groups/blob/247ad87cd7443c1d0f042818e54382fc67dfd75b/active/security.md), formally establishing the Security Team as a DSF Working Group and completing the transition from the former security team structure to the DSF Working Group governance model.
