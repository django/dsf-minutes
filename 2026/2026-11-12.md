## Ongoing business

### Malcolm Tredinnick Memorial Prize

- The Board reviewed the results of the 2026 Malcolm Tredinnick Memorial Prize and is pleased to announce that the prize has been awarded to [Raffaella Suardini](https://raffaella.dev/). Congratulations to Raffaella and all of this year's nominees; the Board received 14 nominations.
- The Board discussed the [announcement](https://www.djangoproject.com/weblog/2026/nov/19/malcolm-prize-awarded-to-raffaella-suardini/) and the impact of the prize in recognizing contributors who welcome and support newcomers to the Django community.

### 2027 DSF Board elections

- The 2027 DSF Board election process has begun, with Priya Pahwa steering the process as Board Secretary.
- Nominations are open for five of the seven Board positions. Members interested in serving on the Board should submit the [2027 DSF Board Nomination Form](https://forms.gle/xnvqAcf7A4xnENH26) by **23:59 on December 2, 2026, [Anywhere on Earth (AoE)](https://www.timeanddate.com/time/zones/aoe)**.
- Questions about the Board role or nomination process may be sent to foundation@djangoproject.com. More information is available in the [DSF board nominations announcement](https://www.djangoproject.com/weblog/2026/nov/15/2027-dsf-board-nominations/).

### Executive Director search

- The Board is in the final stages of hiring an Executive Director and will share an update soon.
- The Board continues to work actively toward finalizing the search, with Jeff Triplett leading much of the day-to-day coordination.
