## Ongoing business

### Executive Director search

- Executive Director application deadline was extended. It is now closed.
- The Board is meeting weekly to work through the hiring process, including interviewing candidates and discussing the next actionables as applicable.
- Things are moving along well, and the Board will share further updates as the process progresses.

### Natalia's sabbatical

- Natalia Bidart is taking a sabbatical from her Django Fellow duties from November through January, and returns in February. You can read her announcement here: [Natalia Bidart - Planned short-term leave](https://www.djangoproject.com/weblog/2026/oct/26/natalia-bidart-planned-short-term-leave/).
- The Board would like to thank Natalia for more than three years of dedicated work as a Django Fellow. We appreciate everything she does for Django and the community, and we hope she gets a well-earned rest.

### Bylaw changes

- Tom Carrick's bylaw changes add language around voting members and affirmation: [dsf-bylaws PR #13](https://github.com/django/dsf-bylaws/pull/13).
- **RESOLVED**, that the Django Software Foundation Board approves the bylaw amendments as proposed in django/dsf-bylaws#13, establishing Voting Members as a defined class, providing that voting status lapses after two consecutive missed annual elections and may be restored in writing, and setting quorum for elections of the Members at a majority of Voting Members.

### Malcolm Tredinnick Memorial Prize

- A quick discussion was done on the [2026 Malcolm Tredinnick Memorial Prize](https://www.djangoproject.com/weblog/2026/oct/01/nominate-someone-for-the-2026-malcolm-prize/), which recognizes a community member who exemplifies Malcolm's spirit of welcoming and supporting newcomers.
- The application deadline is next week. Abigail Afi Gbadago is actively following up on this.

### 2027 DSF Board elections planning

- Priya Pahwa has shared the 2027 DSF Board Elections Planning and Timeline document with the Board for review.
- Board members are asked to review it and share their feedback.

### Website analytics

- We now have analytics running on [djangoproject.com](https://www.djangoproject.com) and [docs.djangoproject.com](https://docs.djangoproject.com).
- This will give us a clearer picture of how people use the website and and will help us make better-informed decisions.

### Press and media kit

- The Board discussed about having a press and media kit, with guidelines and materials that people can use when writing about the DSF or posting about it on social media.
