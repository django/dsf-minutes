## Ongoing business

### Djangonaut Space Grant
- The Django Software Foundation Board approves a grant of $5,000 per year to Djangonaut Space for three years, for a total commitment of $15,000, to support contributor development, conference financial aid and travel, equipment and volunteer expenses, and related program costs.

### DjangoCon US
- The DSF shared a booth with Djangonaut Space.
- The DSF Membership Meeting was held at DjangoCon US and went well.
- The agenda majorly included: Executive Director search, Membership and potential voting changes, Steering Council update + DEP 0020 + DEP 0019 changes

### 2026 Django Developers Survey
- The second PyCharm fundraising campaign has concluded, following an extension of the campaign deadline from September 10 to September 14.
- The 2026 Django Developers Survey results have been published.

### Oracle Sponsorship
- The Oracle sponsorship agreement has been signed and executed.

### DEP 0019 — Technical Governance for the Django Project
- The Board reviewed the latest revision of [DEP 0019 - Technical Governance for the Django project (formerly DEP 0018)](https://github.com/django/deps/compare/3238164583c83b434fb7f7a183d2a8b3f7a22b7c...637cd181d4447492e028cd91e5afa5366ba51dcd).
- [Motion Passed](https://github.com/django/deps/pull/120): RESOLVED, that contingent upon approval of DEP 0019 by the Steering Council, the Django Software Foundation Board supports the changes proposed in DEP 0019, including the relocation of Security Team-specific responsibilities and governance provisions to the Security Team charter or other appropriate governance documents.

### Executive Director Search
- The Board discussed the application review and screening process.
- Applications are scheduled to close on September 14. The Board will assess the applicant pool and determine whether an extension to the deadline is necessary.
- The Board will meet again next week to determine next steps.

## New business

### Sponsorship Plans
- A new sponsorship plans [pull request](https://github.com/django/djangoproject.com/pull/2833) has been proposed to create a more direct sponsorship funnel for prospective sponsors.
- The proposal incorporates feedback following DjangoCon US.
- The Board discussed the possibility of offering a bundled sponsorship plan that would combine multiple sponsorship opportunities.
